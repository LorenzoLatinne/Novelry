# Novelry

A personal reading companion: readers track what they read, set goals, time their reading and get transparent recommendations, without a social layer.

## Language

**Reader**:
The person using the app.
_Avoid_: User, member, account holder

**Book**:
A published work identified by its ISBN, with title, authors, genres, page count and cover.
_Avoid_: Title, volume

**Library**:
A Reader's personal collection of Books, each with a Shelf and progress.
_Avoid_: Bookshelf (as a collection), collection, list

**Library Entry**:
One Book in a Reader's Library, with its Shelf, current page and date added.
_Avoid_: UserBook, item

**Shelf**:
The status of a Library Entry: **Want to read**, **Reading** or **Read**.
_Avoid_: Status, state, Completed, Finished

**Reading Session**:
A timed stretch of reading on one Book, with a start and an end.
_Avoid_: Timer, sitting

**Review**:
A Reader's own rating and comment on a Book, private to the Reader.
_Avoid_: Feedback, opinion

**Rating**:
The score from 1 to 5 a Reader gives a Book within a Review.
_Avoid_: Stars, grade

**Reading Goal**:
A target a Reader sets for themselves: a number of Books per year or minutes read per day.
_Avoid_: Challenge, target, objective

**Achievement**:
A milestone a Reader earns through their reading, such as finishing a number of Books.
_Avoid_: Badge, trophy, reward

**Recommendation**:
A Book suggested to a Reader, ranked on-device from their genres, favorites and reading behavior, with the reason shown.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, tip

**Favorite**:
A Book the Reader rated highly or marked as a favorite, used to shape Recommendations.
_Avoid_: Liked book

**Export**:
A file containing a Reader's whole Library, Reviews, Sessions and Goals, used to move to another phone or back up.
_Avoid_: Backup file, dump

**Scan**:
Looking up a Book by reading the ISBN barcode with the camera.
_Avoid_: Barcode search
